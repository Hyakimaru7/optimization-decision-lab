## Original validation and optimality

## Results

## Ablations, sensitivity, or independent evaluation

Only relevant executed analyses, changed factors, ranges, common application metrics, and stability.

## Practical decisions or research

Action tables, initial/terminal states, commitments, switching and alternatives; or falsifiable hypotheses, identification, surrogate scope, and minimal distinguishing experiments. Unexecuted real actions/experiments remain proposals.

## Limits and reproduction

Accuracy, scale, data, licenses, environment, configuration, raw records, solutions/logs, reproduction, completed work, and remaining gaps.

## Sources
