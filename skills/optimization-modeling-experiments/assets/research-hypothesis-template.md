# Research hypothesis and distinguishing experiment

**English** | [简体中文](zh-CN/research-hypothesis-template.md)

## Question and nearest baseline

Phenomenon, bottleneck, problem class, established model/method, original sources, and search scope.

## Mechanism and proposition

Changed assumption/mechanism, expected effect, and modeling/algorithmic/experimental/application contribution.

## Falsifiable prediction

Main metric, control, expected direction, refuting conditions, predefined versus exploratory additions.

## Minimal distinguishing experiment

Data/instrument/simulator, identification, noise, calls/costs, budgets, initial design, ablation, and independent validation.

## Evidence and next step

Separate derivation, proxy results, actual observations, unresolved conditions, and the most useful distinguishing work.
