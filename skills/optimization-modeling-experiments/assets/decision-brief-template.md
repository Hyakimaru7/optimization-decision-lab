# Decision brief

**English** | [简体中文](zh-CN/decision-brief-template.md)

## Decision

Record actor, controllable actions, decision/execution time, visible information, horizon, and current policy.

## Success

Actual goal, proxy, units, baseline, acceptance criteria, and priorities/weights.

## Conditions and assumptions

Separate hard rules, preferences, and authorized recourse. Explain assumptions, omitted mechanisms, and invalidation conditions.

## Data support

Fields, granularity, units, missingness/duplicates, freshness, bias, sources, missing measurements, and probability/range provenance.

## Validation and delivery

Minimal instance, original checks, relevant scenarios/replay, budgets, action table, and full costs versus current practice.

## Reuse and changes (when relevant)

Requirement/formula/validator links and counterexamples; authentic model/data/context revisions; validity periods, commitments, perturbation ranges, and review actions beyond the range. Records do not replace actual execution checks.

## Next information (when decision-relevant)

States to distinguish, actions affected, timely measurements, cost/delay, observation-model evidence, conditional information value, or minimal distinguishing experiment.
